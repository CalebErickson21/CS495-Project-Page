# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## Why are my changes to the data not reflected on the dashboard?

The dashboard does not update automatically when the underlying sheets are edited. Simply click **Refresh** in the top right corner of the page to generate insights with your updated data.

---

## Why do some numbers show blank or wrong?

Some data may be incorrect, incomplete, or deemed unreliable. In this case, placeholder values or N/A may be used.

---

## How do I access my "Monday Morning Huddle" routine?

Just ask! The AI chatbot integrated into the website is capable of generating interactive summaries to use as a huddle routine.
